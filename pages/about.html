Who We Are

Founded by Jon Juhl and Stephen Johnson, our leadership brings over 30 years of combined, hands-on experience executing complex, mission-critical installations for Fortune 500 campuses, international airports, and major healthcare facilities. We specialize in Structured Cabling, Access Control, CCTV, Security, and Commercial/Public Safety DAS.

Our Story

We started RyComm Technologies for two simple reasons: a genuine passion for the craft and a shared frustration with how the industry often operates. After decades of watching larger firms overpromise and fail to connect planning with field execution, we decided to build something better.

By combining hands-on technical background with high-level project management, we created a specialized installation company designed to eliminate inefficiencies and protect our clients’ budgets. Clients deserve a partner who communicates honestly and cares about the final product as much as they do.
